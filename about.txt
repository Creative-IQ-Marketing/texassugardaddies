Overview of Texas Sugar Daddies
Texas Sugar Daddies is a San Antonio-based bakery and catering business founded in 2017 by two "foodie dads" who left corporate jobs to pursue their passion for creating "epic bites." The company focuses on custom cakes, fresh baked goods, cafe services, catering, and venue rentals, with a mission to bring people together through high-quality food and exceptional service. Their taglines include "Life is short, eat dessert first!" and "Everything's bigger in Texas, including our desserts!" They've served over 500 clients and emphasize premium ingredients, expert staff, and reliable delivery (free for orders over $10).
History and Founders
Started online in 2017, the business is run by two dads committed to 100% "epic bites" and has earned 5-star customer reviews. It highlights their transition from cubicles to a full-service operation, blending bakery expertise with event hosting.
Key Services and Features
The site promotes a range of offerings under "We Do It All!":

Bakery: Custom cakes, cookies, cupcakes, and personalized sugar cookies with custom printing. They specialize in treats for events like birthdays, weddings, and baby showers.
Cafe: Open weekdays (Tuesday-Thursday, 11:00 AM - 3:00 PM) with daily specials, a salad bar, lunch options, and grab-and-go items. Monday-Thursday hours focus on lunch service.
Catering: Custom menus for onsite or offsite events, including weddings, corporate meetings, anniversaries, and parties. They promise professional chefs, on-time delivery, and flawless execution.
Venue Rentals: Space for up to 150 people with full amenities, ideal for hosting events alongside their food services.

Customers can place orders directly through the site, with links for bakery items, catering, and event hosting. No specific menu details or pricing are listed on the homepage, but they encourage contacting them for custom needs.
